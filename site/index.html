How it works

Each new domain or certificate name is compared with every brand. Exact names, look-alike Unicode letters, leetspeak and typos score points, and so do the words phishing kits add (login, colis, facture, remboursement), country hints and heavily abused TLDs. Every point is written down, so you can see exactly why a domain was flagged. Official domains are never flagged.

The watch is passive: it reads public feeds and certificate logs and never visits, scans or touches a suspected site. Report anything you confirm to the brand and to your national CERT.
